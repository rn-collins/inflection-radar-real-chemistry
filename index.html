Will’s five questions

The report answers the diligence points explicitly before asking anyone to read technical details.

AI model

Model-task portfolio

AI assists bounded tasks: extraction, comparison, entity-resolution support, classification, contradiction detection and draft synthesis. Human review owns inclusion, interpretation, scoring and portfolio decisions.

Assumptions

Visible, not hidden

Scope, evidence thresholds, weights, recency windows and service-fit logic are kept in an assumption register so reviewers can challenge them.

Permissions

Source path governs use

Each source is evaluated for acquisition, storage, transformation, model use, audience, retention and reuse. Public access is not treated as blanket permission.

Compliance

Company-level perimeter

Pilot 1 operates at the company and market level and does not require patient-level data or PHI. Safety or product-complaint information is routed under Real Chemistry’s approved process if encountered.

Insights

Evidence-proportionate conclusions

The system distinguishes verified facts, corroborated findings, analytical inferences and early signals. Real Chemistry retains the pursuit decision.

What makes an insight reliable enough to circulate?

Reliability is demonstrated through traceability, reviewer agreement, sensitivity testing and explicit limits—not asserted from a model name.

Record accuracy

Sampled extracted fields are compared with the underlying source and locator. Material fields require human validation.

Entity precision

False merges and false splits are measured across companies, subsidiaries, assets and renamed programs.

Classification agreement

Two-reviewer agreement tests whether the ontology produces consistent signal labels and evidence tiers.

Score sensitivity

Priority order is retested under plausible weight and missing-data changes. Fragile rankings are labelled.

False-positive review

High-scoring accounts are actively challenged for self-promotional evidence, stale events and weak service-need logic.

Decision usefulness

Internal reviewers assess novelty, actionability, duplication, routing clarity and the cost of reviewing each account.

Pilot 1 answers which companies appear externally attractive for review. It cannot establish conflicts, relationships, economics, ownership, capacity or whether Real Chemistry should pursue them.
